# Anatel Resolução nº 789/2026

Checked 18 October 2026 (effective-date calculation added 21 October 2026) against the [official Anatel text](https://informacoes.anatel.gov.br/legislacao/resolucoes/2026/2157-resolucao-789). This is a summary of the provisions used in the comparison.

| Date or provision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 6 July 2026 | Signing date |
| 13 July 2026 | DOU publication date stated on the official page |
| Article 5 | Takes effect 30 days counted from publication; the signing date is not the effective date |
| 12 August 2026 | Effective date computed under LC 95/1998 Art. 8 §1 (13 July = day 1, 11 August = day 30, in force the next day). Plain date addition (13 July + 30 days) gives the same day. Anatel does not state the calendar date |
| Article 2 / RCC-SRA Article 3 / Table II | Second-generation TV uses VHF and UHF; the table includes 174–216, 250–298, 298–322, 470–608 and 614–698 MHz |
| Article 2 / RCC-SRA Article 7 / Table IV | Channelization distinguishes first-generation TVD and second-generation TV 3.0 |

The 250–322 MHz additions must not be omitted from a description of this regulation. They do not mean every listed sub-band or channel is available for every individual station; the channel plan and licensing conditions still apply.

Portaria MCom nº 10.693/2023 is a separate earlier spectrum-policy instrument. Its signing, publication and effective dates should be recorded separately. Re-checked 21 October 2026: the official MCom SERAD register lists publication on 19 October 2023 (DOU nº 199, Seção 1, p.10), and the official DOU search extract of Art. 3 puts the ordinance in force on publication.
