# Anatel Resolução nº 789/2026

Checked 18 October 2026 against the [official Anatel text](https://informacoes.anatel.gov.br/legislacao/resolucoes/2026/2157-resolucao-789). This is a summary of the provisions used in the comparison.

| Date or provision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 6 July 2026 | Signing date |
| 13 July 2026 | DOU publication date stated on the official page |
| Article 5 | Takes effect 30 days counted from publication; the signing date is not the effective date |
| Article 2 / RCC-SRA Article 3 / Table II | Second-generation TV uses VHF and UHF; the table includes 174–216, 250–298, 298–322, 470–608 and 614–698 MHz |
| Article 2 / RCC-SRA Article 7 / Table IV | Channelization distinguishes first-generation TVD and second-generation TV 3.0 |

The 250–322 MHz additions must not be omitted from a description of this regulation. They do not mean every listed sub-band or channel is available for every individual station; the channel plan and licensing conditions still apply.

Portaria MCom nº 10.693/2023 is a separate earlier spectrum-policy instrument. Its signing, publication and effective dates should be recorded separately. The full official ordinance text could not be retrieved during this review, so its publication/effective dates remain unverified.
